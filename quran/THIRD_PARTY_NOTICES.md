# Third-party components and sources

- Quran Uthmani text: https://api.alquran.cloud/v1/quran/quran-uthmani (Al Quran Cloud, edition `quran-uthmani`). Original text is preserved; source response fingerprint is in `assets/quran-source.json`. Underlying text credited to Tanzil, https://tanzil.net/docs/Text_License (CC BY 3.0; verbatim distribution, attribution and link retained).
- Recitation audio: https://everyayah.com/recitations_ayat.html. Files are fetched on demand from https://everyayah.com/data/ and are attributed to their named reciters. One Husary 2:3 sample is bundled. This attribution does not grant rights beyond each recording's applicable terms.
- Badr Al-Turki ayah clips: QUD³ Technologies, Qur'anic Universal Ayahs, subset `badr_al_turki_yt`, https://huggingface.co/datasets/QUD-Technologies/quranic-universal-ayahs . Audio is requested on demand through the Dataset Viewer rows API, https://huggingface.co/docs/dataset-viewer/rows . QUA segmentation, alignment and metadata are CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ ; recording rights remain with the reciter and upstream sources. The app checks the original dataset's chapter and verse identifiers and uses its supplied clips without altering their content. The local Quran text is not replaced by dataset text. Project source and attribution: https://github.com/QUD-Technologies/quranic-universal-audio .
- Cairo: Google Fonts / Mohamed Gaber and contributors, SIL Open Font License 1.1. https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/cairo
- Amiri Quran: Khaled Hosny and contributors, SIL Open Font License 1.1. https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/amiriquran
- FFmpeg JS wrapper @ffmpeg/ffmpeg 0.12.15, MIT. https://github.com/ffmpegwasm/ffmpeg.wasm/tree/v0.12.15
- FFmpeg core @ffmpeg/core 0.12.10; accompanying FFmpeg and included codecs are under their respective LGPL/GPL licenses. GPL-enabled libx264 is used in this build. Source, build scripts, and exact release: https://github.com/ffmpegwasm/ffmpeg.wasm/tree/v0.12.10 and https://github.com/ffmpegwasm/ffmpeg.wasm/tree/v0.12.10/packages/core. FFmpeg source: https://ffmpeg.org/download.html; libx264 source: https://code.videolan.org/videolan/x264. License texts are provided in `licenses/`. The WASM binary is unmodified; partitioning is reversible byte-for-byte as specified by `assets/ffmpeg/wasm.json`.
- Nature photos: Unsplash, https://unsplash.com/license. Source image identifiers: photo-1464822759023-fed622ff2c3b, photo-1473580044384-7ba9967e16a0, photo-1519681393784-d120267933ba. The motion preset is a prepared, gently animated version of the night photo.

No watermark or source labels are added to exported Quran videos; these notices remain with the app distribution.
